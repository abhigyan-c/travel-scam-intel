Travel Scam Intelligence

Travel Scam Intelligence helps travelers research reported scam patterns, inspect source evidence, and anonymously report incidents in ChatGPT.

Published by abhigyan-c. English only. The product has no payments or purchases.

What you can do

  • Ask about a city, country or attraction, with country context when names are ambiguous.
  • Request a sourced report and follow one bounded research run.
  • Inspect evidence, confidence, actual location and missing dates.
  • Review and confirm an anonymous incident report before submitting it.

Try a travel question

Use Travel Scam Intelligence to check scams in Bangkok, Thailand. I am visiting next month. Give me a sourced report and practical precautions. Do not submit an incident report.

The product is being prepared for public ChatGPT review. A private development connection is not a public directory listing. Availability will depend on OpenAI approval and supported accounts, regions and interfaces.

Understand the limits

Results can be partial, outdated, low-confidence or empty. An allegation is not a verified incident. Confidence is an evidence-ranking score, not the probability that a claim is true. A finding at one attraction does not establish a citywide problem. No result does not mean a destination is safe.

This service does not provide emergency assistance, guarantees of safety, bookings or financial transactions. In an emergency, contact appropriate local services.
