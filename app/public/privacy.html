Privacy policy

Prepared for release 0.1.0, updated November 22, 2026. Publisher: abhigyan-c. Contact: abhigyanchakraborty61@gmail.com.

Information processed

The service receives the tool inputs needed for your request: locations, incident descriptions you ask it to compare, optional incident dates, provenance choices, supporting text/links and confirmed anonymous submissions. It does not require a consumer account or request access to your entire ChatGPT conversation. ChatGPT determines which tool arguments it sends.

Confirmed submissions produce database records, receipt identifiers, hashed idempotency keys and private raw objects. Common identifiers may be redacted automatically and flagged text may be held for review, but filters cannot detect every sensitive value. Do not submit contact details, credentials, payment information, health information or government identifiers. The original private submission may remain in raw storage even when its normalized text is redacted.

Public-source research stores source URLs, fetched content, excerpts, normalized claims, locations, evidence links and source-quality information. Eligible derived findings and evidence excerpts can be shown to other users. Report-derived evidence may also become public after the applicable processing and moderation safeguards. Raw report objects and private attachments are not public source links.

Purpose and recipients

Information supports research, evidence attribution, comparison, anonymous reporting, abuse controls and administration. Search queries go to Serper; selected source/report-derived text and context may go to Sarvam for classification and extraction. Embeddings are generated locally. Hosting/storage use DigitalOcean and private Cloudflare R2; Bitwarden stores operational credentials and Tailscale provides network access. External source websites receive server-side fetch requests. Provider and network services can process connection metadata under their own policies.

The app does not store text supplied solely for incident comparison as a user report. Accepting a report is a separate confirmed action. Application logs retain request IDs, route templates, status and timings; operational storage includes aggregate counters, location demand and research/job history. The service does not use a consumer account system or an advertising profile. OpenAI separately processes ChatGPT activity under its own terms and privacy controls.

Retention

There is currently no automatic expiry for accepted reports, source evidence, raw objects, database history or encrypted backups. They may be retained indefinitely. Draft incident forms remain in the open view and closing that view discards its draft. Closing the view or disconnecting the app does not delete an accepted report. Operational counters and abuse-control buckets have short expiries; these do not delete the underlying submissions or research records.

Controls, corrections and deletion requests

You choose whether to submit an incident report and can edit it before confirmation. Contact support for correction, removal or privacy requests, preferably with a receipt or source reference. Requests are manually assessed; the product has no self-service account deletion or guaranteed automatic erasure workflow. Withholding a finding is not physical deletion of all copies. Backups can retain earlier copies, and a restore may reintroduce data unless the operator reapplies relevant removals. Do not assume a fixed deletion deadline.

Security and changes

Admin records require a private network path and bearer token. Raw objects and backups use private buckets; backups are encrypted. These controls cannot guarantee security or anonymity against every risk. This policy should be reread when updated; material data-practice changes must be reflected before the changed release is published.
